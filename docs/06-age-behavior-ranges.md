## Key research facts (what the sources say)

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Newborn feed frequency | 8–12 feeds / 24 h. Formula: ~1–2 oz every 2–3 h in the first days. Breastfed: every 1–3 h early on. Don't exceed ~2–3 h by day / 4 h at night | S1, S3, S5 |
| Formula progression | ~3–4 oz every 3–4 h by end of month 1. Then +~1 oz/month, levelling at ~7–8 oz per feed. ~32 oz/day cap | S2 |
| 6 months | ~6–8 oz × 4–5 feeds / 24 h *(verify: excerpt attributed to a summary)* | S2 |
| 6–12 months | Formula or solids ~5–6 times / 24 h *(verify)*. Solids start ~6 months. Milk remains main nutrition to 12 m | S1, S23 |
| Breastfeed duration | ~10 min first breast, up to 15 min second (early). Up to 20 min first breast once established | S4 |
| Burping | Bottle: every 2–3 oz. Breast: when switching sides | S16 |
| Sleep 0–3 m | 14–17 h / 24 h recommended (11–13 / 18–19 "may be appropriate") | S6 |
| Sleep < 4 m | AASM gives **no** recommendation due to wide normal variation | S7 |
| Sleep 4–12 m | 12–16 h / 24 h incl. naps | S7 |
| Sleep 1–2 y | 11–14 h / 24 h incl. naps | S7 |
| Night wakings | ~1.7/night at 0–2 m, ~0.8 at 3–6 m; 1–2 y up to 2.5 in some studies | S8 |
| Uninterrupted night sleep | 6 m: 62% ≥ 6 h, 43% ≥ 8 h. 12 m: 72% ≥ 6 h, 57% ≥ 8 h | S9 |
| Sleep consolidation | Largest gains 1–4 m. Plateau 4–9 m. Lengthens again 10–12 m | S10 |
| Normal night waking 6 m | Normal to wake and resettle within minutes | S22 |
| Sleep cycles | Newborn ~50–60 min (active + quiet sleep). Circadian rhythm emerges ~2–3 m, not mature until after 1 y | S17 |
| Crying 0–6 wk | 117–133 min fuss/cry per 24 h (SD ~66–70) | S11 |
| Crying 10–12 wk | ~68 min / 24 h (SD 46). No universal 6-week peak | S11 |
| Colic prevalence | 17–25% (0–6 wk) → 11% (8–9 wk) → 0.6% (10–12 wk) | S11 |
| Crying timing | Clusters in late afternoon/evening in early months | S12 |
| Wet diapers | ≥ 6/day with pale urine by day 5–7 | S13 |
| Stools | ~3+/day by day 5 *(S, commercial)*. May drop to 1–2/day by ~6 wk | S24, S14 |
| Vocal milestones | 2 m: sounds other than crying. 4 m: coos ("oooo/aahh"). 6 m: consonant jabbering (m, b). 9 m: "mamamama/babababa". 12 m: waves bye-bye | S15 |
| Separation anxiety | Begins ~8 m, often strongest ~9 m | S20 |
| Tantrums | Most common 1–3 y | S21 |
| Naps | Two naps typical by 10–12 m. 2 → 1 between 13–18 m (parent-reported dataset) | S18 |
| Wake windows | **No peer-reviewed age-specific evidence found.** Popular charts are heuristics | S19 |
